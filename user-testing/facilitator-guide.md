# Facilitator guide (25–30 min)

## Before they join

- Open the product (or Figma) in a clean state — private window if using a live link.
- Open [notes-template.md](./notes-template.md) for this participant.
- Confirm recording preference from consent.

## 0:00–2:00 · Warm-up

> Thanks for joining. I’m testing the product, not you. Thinking out loud helps a lot — say what you’re looking for, what you expect, what feels unclear. You can stop anytime.

Ask:

1. Have you used anything like a journal, mood tracker, or goals app before?
2. What does “self-care app” mean to you in one sentence?

## 2:00–5:00 · First impression (no tasks yet)

Show or open the starting screen (onboarding if first visit).

> Without tapping yet — what is this? Who’s it for? What would you do first?

Note: words they use vs your labels (Garden, Intentions, etc.).

## 5:00–22:00 · Tasks

Read tasks from [tasks.md](./tasks.md) one at a time.  
Don’t teach unless they’re stuck for ~45 seconds — then give a small hint and mark **needed help**.

After each task: “How easy was that, 1–5?” (1 hard → 5 easy)

## 22:00–28:00 · Wrap-up

1. What felt clearest?
2. What felt confusing or heavy?
3. Would you open this again tomorrow morning? Why / why not?
4. If you could rename one thing, what would it be?
5. Anything missing you expected?

## 28:00–30:00 · Thanks

> This was really helpful. Here’s how to reach me if anything else comes to mind: [contact].

## Facilitator tips

- Silence is okay — count to 5 before helping.
- Prefer “What are you trying to do?” over “Click there.”
- Capture **quotes** verbatim when they sound emotional or stuck.
- Don’t defend the design mid-session.
