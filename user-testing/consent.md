# Consent (copy into a form or message)

You’re invited to a short usability session for **Glow Within**.

## What happens

- You’ll try the product (or design screens) while talking through what you notice.
- The facilitator may ask follow-up questions. There are no right or wrong answers.
- Sessions last about **25–30 minutes**.

## Privacy

- Glow Within keeps data in **your browser only** (local storage). It does not require an account and does not sync to a server in this version.
- Session notes are for product improvement. They won’t be shared publicly with your name unless you agree.
- If we record (audio/video/screen), we’ll say so before starting. You can ask to stop recording anytime or do **notes-only**.

## Voluntary

You can skip any task or end the session at any time.

## Agreement

By joining the session, you confirm:

- [ ] You’re 18+ (or have guardian permission if required)  
- [ ] You understand the session is for feedback, not evaluation of you  
- [ ] You’re okay with notes being taken
