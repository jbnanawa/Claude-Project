# Participant tasks

Read one at a time. Don’t reveal the next task early.

---

### Task 1 — Get started  
**Scenario:** You’re trying this app for the first time.  
**Ask:** Set yourself up so the app feels like yours.

*Success:* Completes onboarding (name ± focus areas) and lands on Today.

---

### Task 2 — Orient  
**Ask:** Look around Today. In your own words, what can you do here? Where would you go to write about your day?

*Success:* Mentions affirmation / growth / goals / vision *or* finds Garden without being told the name.

---

### Task 3 — Daily check-in (Garden)  
**Ask:** Check in for today — leave at least one gratitude.

*Success:* Saves a Garden entry for today (“plant” / water flow).

---

### Task 4 — Intentions  
**Ask:** Set a focus for this month and at least one priority you want to tend.

*Success:* Saves monthly intentions with focus + ≥1 priority.

---

### Task 5 — Vision board  
**Ask:** Add one image to your vision board (link or upload) and give it a short meaning.

*Success:* One vision item appears on the board.

---

### Task 6 (optional) — Find your way back  
**Ask:** Go back to Today without using the browser back button. Add or open something related to your goals/intentions if it feels natural.

*Success:* Uses nav (top or bottom tabs) confidently.

---

## Difficulty scale (after each task)

**1** = couldn’t do it · **3** = got there with effort · **5** = easy / obvious
