One-line: The signature translucent glass card — the container for nearly every panel in Glow Within.

```jsx
<Card blobs>
  <h2 style={{fontFamily:"var(--font-serif)"}}>Your Growth Journey</h2>
  <p>Each check-in helps your garden grow.</p>
</Card>
```

`blobs` adds the soft blush + sage orbs seen in the affirmation/journey cards. 24px radius, 32px padding by default. Backdrop-blurred, so it reads best over the aura page background.
